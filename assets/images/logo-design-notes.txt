CoffeeLog logo v3: Bean Archive Mark

MCP researcher/web-searcher feedback converged on the same issue: the previous mark tried to explain too many coffee actions at once. The final direction keeps one coffee bean as the recognition object and one small cream archive tab as the inventory/logging cue.

Production rules:
- No text, cup, steam, droplet, saucer, outer border, or notebook lines.
- One large tilted bean, one thick S-shaped crease, one label tab.
- Dark espresso background, warm roasted bean, cream inventory tab.
- Critical details stay thick enough to survive a 48px launcher preview.
